How to label (1 minute)
Ask one question: is this good or bad news for ordinary US households? Judge the text as written; your politics don't matter.
Positive: “Inflation cools to 2.4%”, “Economy adds 300,000 jobs”, “Gas prices fall”. Negative: “Layoffs surge”, “Grocery prices skyrocket”, “Mortgage rates climb”. Neutral: “Fed holds rates steady”, “What the CPI report means for you”, mixed or unclear.
Not about the US economy: another country's economy, ads, sports, spam, unrelated topics.
Order: Quick 100 (about 10 minutes) → optionally, later, the Re-check 30, which repeats some items in a new order to measure consistency. Don't look back at earlier answers.
